About the Priority Score

Priority Scores are calculated from each tree's condition rating and recorded structural observations, including defects such as stem cavities, co-dominant stems, included bark, and similar indicators. Scores range from 0–100; lower scores indicate more urgent attention. This is a planning and triage tool — it is not a formal ISA TRAQ risk assessment.
